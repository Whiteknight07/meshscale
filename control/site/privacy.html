meshscale

Privacy

meshscale is open-source software that runs on your own devices. Its maintainers operate no service that receives your data.

What stays on your device

When you sign in with Cloudflare, the OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally in a file readable only by your user (or by root for the system service). They are sent only to Cloudflare's API, to manage your own account.

What goes to Cloudflare

meshscale calls Cloudflare's API on your behalf, and your network traffic is carried by Cloudflare's official client. Cloudflare's privacy policy covers that processing.

What meshscale does not do

No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or tracking. meshscale bugreport writes a redacted archive to your disk and never uploads it.

This website

This static site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Cloudflare serves it and may keep standard request logs.

Contact

Open an issue on GitHub. For security reports, see SECURITY.md.
